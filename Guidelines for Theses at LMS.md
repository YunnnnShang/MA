
# Guidelines for Theses at LMS
## General Rules
### Report Structure
* Use LaTeX template: https://gitlab.lms.tf.fau.de/LMS/LMS-Bericht-LaTeX
* Common agenda
    1. Introduction
    2. State of the Art
    3. Idea/Concept
    4. Implementation/Realization
    5. Results/Evaluation
    6. Conclusion/Outlook
* Amount of pages (only text, without tables of content, appendix, etc.)
    * Bachelor's thesis: ca. 40-60 pages
    * Master's thesis: ca. 70-100 pages
* After table of content: german "Kurzfassung" AND english abstract, approx. one page
* Subsequently, list of used symbols and abbreviations is required
* After the last chapter include: List of Images, List of Tables, References
* For Master Thesis: Include a curriculum vitae written of about 1 page which should not be
included in the Table of Contents
* Every result from the master thesis that is not directly used in the main part of the thesis
should be put into the appendix

### Outer Form Report 
* Format: DIN A5, printed duplex
* Transparent front cover, black back for Master's thesis, gray back for Bachelor's thesis 
* Hand in three copies which you can print at the chair
* Adhesive binding

### Final Presentation
* Can be hold in English or German
* Master's thesis: 30 minutes + questions by the audience
* Every other thesis: 20 minutes + questions by the audience
* Use LMS template from GitLab
    * For PowerPoint: https://gitlab.lms.tf.fau.de/LMS/LMS-Praesentationen-Powerpoint
    * For LaTeX: https://gitlab.lms.tf.fau.de/LMS/LMS-Praesentationen-LaTeX

## Checklist before Hand Over
### Outer Form
* Do all chapters start on a right hand side (odd page number)? -> If not, insert empty pages
* Are section and subsection headlines correctly shown in the Table of Contents or do they
span over more than one line? -> Use short titles for the Table of Contents
* Are there sections with just one subsection? -> If yes, either try to rearrange these or expand
the sections, such that there are at least two subsections in one section, otherwise it is
unnecessary
* No abbreviations in headlines!
* Do Figures/Tables with longer captions have a short caption?
* Are the references formatted tidy?
* Are there any errors in the title page?
* Is the signed proposal included on page 3 (first right side)?
* Is the curriculum vitae included but taken out of the Table of Contents?

### Continuous Text
* Spellcheck?
* Are there any colloquial expressions left?
* Checked all commas?
* Are there any paragraphs broken in an unfavorable way?

### Figures
* Are all figures referred?
* Are all figures (if possible) in vector format?
* Do all figures have meaningful captions?
* Do all figures have a short title for the list of Figures?
* Are all figures arranged to not be wider or higher than the text?
* Fonts in Figures should at least have a size of 12pt?
* Are diagrams distinguishable in black and white?

### References
* Are all references formatted identically?
* Does every reference have a publication date?
* Were unnecessary DOI and ISBN-Numbers removed
* Were all online sources checked close to handing in the thesis
